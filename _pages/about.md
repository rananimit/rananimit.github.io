<p>
I am a Lecturer in the Department of Mathematics at the <a href="https://www.york.ac.uk/maths/" target="_blank">University of York</a>. 
</p>

<p>
My research lies at the intersection of probability theory, stochastic analysis, rough path theory, and modern machine learning. I am particularly interested in the theoretical foundations and algorithmic innovations involved in the study of classical and singular stochastic partial differential equations. 
</p>

<p>
In parallel, I apply stochastic optimal control, reinforcement learning, rough path methods, and infinite-dimensional deep learning to quantitative finance, with the aim of developing data-driven models with mathematical and statistical guarantees that capture the complex dynamics and behaviour of real-world financial markets, including their microstructure, and support risk management, portfolio optimisation, and financial decision-making. 
</p>

<p>
I obtained an MSc in Mathematical Sciences from <a href="https://maths.iisertvm.ac.in/" target="_blank">IISER Thiruvananthapuram</a>, India, followed by a PhD in stochastic (geometric) PDEs at the University of York, UK, under the supervision of <a href="https://www.york.ac.uk/maths/people/zdzislaw-brzezniak/" target="_blank">Zdzisław Brzeźniak</a>. I subsequently held research associate positions at Universität Bielefeld, Germany, in the group of <a href="https://ekvv.uni-bielefeld.de/pers_publ/publ/PersonDetail.jsp?personId=106484011&lang=EN" target="_blank">Martina Hofmanová</a>, and later at Imperial College London, UK, in the group of <a href="https://www.hairer.org/" target="_blank">Martin Hairer</a>.</p> 

</div>
